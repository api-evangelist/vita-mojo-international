# Vita Mojo International

> Vita Mojo International is a London-based hospitality technology company behind VMOS, an end-to-end order management system for quick-service restaurants that unifies point of sale, self-service kiosks, digital ordering, click-and-collect, kitchen management, menu management, loyalty, and data analytics (including the Scout AI analyst). Launched in 2015 and backed by Battery Ventures, it powers 3,100+ locations for brands such as Subway, GAIL's, LEON, Honest Burgers, and YO!, primarily in the UK and Netherlands.

Vita Mojo's integration surface is partner-oriented: a partner-gated Reporting API
(access requires a VM account with a Reporting role), an EPOS Connector that
delivers VMOS orders via webhooks in supported formats, and a directory of
technology partner integrations. No public API reference or OpenAPI is published.
Vita Mojo publishes its own structured company summary for AI assistants (linked
below). First-party JavaScript libraries are public on npm under @vita-mojo.
